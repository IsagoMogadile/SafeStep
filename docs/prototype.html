Privacy notice

What we collect: your location during an active alert or journey, trusted contact details, and any reports you submit.

Why: to notify responders and your trusted contacts quickly during an emergency.

Who can access it: campus security only during an active alert, and admin for reports and safety planning.

Retention: alert and location data is kept only as long as needed for follow-up, per POPIA.

This is a hackathon prototype. All data shown is simulated. In an emergency, always contact official services directly.
